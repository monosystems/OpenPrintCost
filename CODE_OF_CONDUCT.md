# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be factual, precise, and respectful. Technical correctness matters more than
polite phrasing — but never at the expense of people.

We welcome contributors regardless of experience level, background, or
identity. Disagreement is normal; demeaning others, harassment, or blocking
contributions instead of improving them is not acceptable here.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and any other interaction in
this repository.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove offending content and, for repeated or severe cases,
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports are handled
confidentially; retaliation against reporters is itself a violation.

This is a lightweight project-specific code. It is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html)
without adopting it verbatim.
